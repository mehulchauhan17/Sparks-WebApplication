# SPARK — 2-minute assessment presentation

*(Speak it; don't read it. Have the home page open as Priya.)*

**[0:00 – Problem]**
Every engineering group has the same two things sitting next to each other and never meeting:
managers with a two-week problem and no spare hands, and engineers with skills nobody outside
their team knows about. Today that gap is closed by hallway conversations — if at all.

**[0:20 – Solution]**
SPARK is an internal opportunity marketplace. A manager posts a short, well-scoped need.
Employees discover it, apply in two minutes, collaborate, deliver, and get recognised.
Discover, apply, collaborate, deliver, recognise — one loop, and every screen pushes you
to the next step of it.

**[0:40 – Workflow, live]**
Here's Priya. Spark AI already tells her the AI Productivity Accelerator is a 97% match.
She opens it — lifecycle at OPEN, what she'll work on, why it matters, the mentor. She applies.
*(switch to Arjun)* Arjun gets the e-mail; she's top of his review list, ranked by fit.
He opens her profile, sees the skill breakdown, and accepts. The project flips to ONGOING
by itself. When it's done he marks it complete, records the impact, and awards Innovation
Champion. *(switch to Priya)* She sees it in her notifications and on the recognition wall,
and the home page already reflects all of it.

**[1:20 – Architecture]**
Nothing you saw needs custom infrastructure. This *(click "Behind the scenes")* is the point:
each action is a Microsoft Lists write and a Power Automate flow — Forms in, Outlook and Teams
out. Five lists, five flows, SharePoint pages on top, Entra ID for identity. The architecture
page in the product lists the columns and the flows; phase one is a two-week build.

**[1:45 – AI and close]**
Spark AI is rule-based today so the demo is reliable; the data model is already what Copilot
Studio and Azure AI need — an "Ask Spark" agent in Teams next, semantic matching after that.
The business case is simple: hidden talent gets found, cross-team work gets staffed in days,
managers spend minutes not weeks, and contribution gets recognised where everyone can see it.
That's SPARK.
